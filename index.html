Warning Signs

Is your foundation
trying to tell you something?

Texas clay soil expands and shrinks with the weather — and it pulls your home with it. Catching these signs early saves you tens of thousands.

01

Cracks in Walls
or Ceilings

Especially diagonal cracks above doors and windows — the classic foundation movement signal.

02

Sticking Doors
& Windows

If they used to open fine and now they fight you, your frame has shifted with the foundation.

03

Sloped or Uneven
Floors

Drop a marble — does it roll? That's gravity telling you what your eyes can't.

04

Gaps Around
Window Frames

Visible separation between trim and walls means the structure is pulling apart.
